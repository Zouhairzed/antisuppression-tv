Antisuppression Bars Privacy Policy

For the LG webOS TV app “Antisuppression Bars” · Effective 11 October 2026

In short: the app does not collect, send or share any personal data. Everything it remembers stays on your TV.

What the app stores

To remember your preferences, the app saves a small amount of information in the TV’s local app storage:

  • Your bar settings: orientation, bar width, bar strength and red/green shades
  • Your text size for the reading screen
  • Whether you have seen the first-run notice
  • Your best score in each game

This information never leaves your TV. It is not linked to your name, LG account or any other identifier.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal information, health information, contacts or location.
  • It does not use the camera or microphone.
  • It does not connect to any server, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app from your TV removes everything it stored.

Children

The app is suitable for children using it with a parent or eye-care professional. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Medical notice

Antisuppression Bars is a supplement to vision therapy prescribed by an optometrist or ophthalmologist. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

LG services

Downloading and running apps on your TV is handled by LG’s own platform and LG Content Store, which have their own privacy policies. This policy covers only the Antisuppression Bars app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For questions, use the developer contact listed on the app’s page in LG Content Store.
